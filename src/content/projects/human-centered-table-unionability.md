This work studies what human and model judgments reveal about whether tables can be meaningfully combined. It examines confidence, decision time, and interaction traces as additional evidence for evaluating labels and supporting union search.

The [TUNE benchmark data](https://github.com/NinaKlimenkova/TUNE_Benchmark/tree/main/data) is available on GitHub.
